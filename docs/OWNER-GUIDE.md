# Editing your website

## What this is

This guide shows you how to update your own website. You add and edit projects, change your About and Contact details, and swap pictures, all through a simple online editor called Pages CMS. Nothing you do here can break the site for good. Every change is kept in history, and Bhanu can bring anything back.

## Signing in

1. Go to https://app.pagescms.org.
2. Click **Sign in with GitHub** and sign in with your GitHub account.
3. Pick your site from the list.

The editor opens with a sidebar on the left showing three sections: **Projects**, **Site settings** and **Media**.

## Adding a project

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Click **Add**.
3. Fill in the fields below. Three are required: Title, Category and Description.
4. Click **Save**.

What each field means:

- **Title** (required). The name of the film, article, exhibition or workshop. If another project already has the same title, add the year or the client to this one so the two stay distinct.
- **Category** (required). Which tab the project appears under. Choose one of the ten: Films, Series, News Features, Music Video, Reels, Writing, Exhibitions, Workshops & Teaching, Improv, Cyanotypes.
- **Client or publisher**. Who it was made for or published by. Leave blank for personal work.
- **Description** (required). One or two sentences. This is what visitors read on the card.
- **Your role**. For example: Director, Editor.
- **Year**. Four digits, for example 2024. Projects are ordered newest first.
- **Link to the work**. The YouTube, Vimeo, article or exhibition page. It must start with http:// or https://. It opens in a new tab.
- **Link button label**. Leave blank and the button says Watch, Read or View automatically.
- **Thumbnail**. The picture on the card. See "Adding or replacing an image" below. If you leave it empty, the card shows the title in large type on a plain background. That looks intentional, not broken.
- **Tags**. Only used on the Writing tab. The Writing tab shows filter buttons made from these tags, and a new tag appears there automatically.
- **Manual order**. Lower numbers appear first within the category. Leave blank to sort by year, newest first.

## Editing or removing a project

To edit:

1. Click **Projects** in the sidebar.
2. Find the project. You can search by title, category, client, description or role.
3. Click it to open the form.
4. Change what you need and click **Save**.

To remove:

1. Open the project as above.
2. Click **Delete** and confirm.

If you delete something by mistake, message Bhanu. It can be brought back.

## Adding or replacing an image

1. Open the project (or **Site settings** for your portrait).
2. Click the **Thumbnail** field.
3. Choose a picture from your computer, or pick one already uploaded.
4. Click **Save**.

A few things to know:

- JPEG only (.jpg or .jpeg). A .webp file also works. PNG files are refused.
- Landscape pictures work best. The card crops to a 16:9 shape, like a widescreen TV.
- Big files are fine. They are resized automatically after upload.
- Keep your own originals on your computer. The site keeps only the resized copy.

To replace a picture, open the project, click the Thumbnail field and choose a different one. To browse everything uploaded so far, click **Media** in the sidebar.

## Editing About, Education and Contact

1. Click **Site settings** in the sidebar.
2. Scroll to the section you want. The sections are, in order: Hero, About, Education, Contact, Work section, Footer, Search and browser.
3. Change the text.
4. Click **Save**.

Notes:

- **Hero** is the top of the home page: your name, the short intro and your portrait.
- **About** has a lead paragraph and any further paragraphs.
- **Education** is a list. Each entry has Degree, Institution and Year. Click an entry to open it.
- **Contact** holds your heading, phone, email and Instagram handle. Enter the handle without the @.
- **Search and browser** sets the words shown in the browser tab and in search engine results.

## After you save

1. Wait about two to three minutes.
2. Open your website address (above) and do a hard refresh. On a Mac, hold Shift and click the reload button, or press Cmd+Shift+R.
3. Check your change.

A hard refresh matters because otherwise your browser may show you an old copy of the page.

Nothing is lost if something looks wrong. Every change is kept in history and Bhanu can bring anything back.

## When something looks wrong

Work through these in order:

1. Wait five minutes and hard-refresh again.
2. Check that you pressed **Save**. Open the item in the editor and see whether your change is there.
3. If a picture is missing, open the project and add the Thumbnail again.
4. If it has still not changed, message Bhanu with the project name and what you expected to see.

If the editor itself will not load, message Bhanu.
